Rosa e Romeo Apartment

On the ground floor, with a south-facing terrace and light from the hills.

Your stay

A home filled with light.

Rosa e Romeo is on the ground floor, accessed through the main entrance. It has a spacious equipped kitchen, a living area with a sofa bed, a double bedroom and a private bathroom.

The bedroom opens onto a private south-facing terrace overlooking the hills. A lovely place for breakfast or a quiet moment outdoors.

The property describes this apartment as suitable for guests with disabilities. Please contact us to check whether the entrance and bathroom meet your specific needs.
