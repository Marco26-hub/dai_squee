Suite Max Apartment

Two levels, handcrafted details and a private terrace in Canova's hometown.

Your stay

Space, character and a view to remember.

Suite Max welcomes up to four guests across two levels. The first floor has an equipped kitchen, a spacious double bedroom and a bathroom.

A stone staircase leads to the second bedroom beneath the wooden roof and to the private terrace. Vintage furniture and handcrafted pieces preserve the house's individual character.

The private sun terrace looks towards the Canova Temple and the mountains: a place to sit outside, read or wait for sunset.

  • Up to 4 guests
  • Two double bedrooms
  • Two levels with an internal staircase
  • Private terrace and sun deck
  • Equipped kitchen
  • Private bathroom and satellite TV
