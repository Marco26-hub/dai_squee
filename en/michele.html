Michele Apartment

The warmth of original wooden floors and open views of the hills.

Your stay

Simply feel at home.

Michele is on the first floor of Dai Squee. Traditional details and original wooden floors give the apartment a welcoming, intimate atmosphere.

The double bedroom, equipped kitchen and private bathroom offer independence throughout your stay. A sofa bed provides additional sleeping space; please confirm guest capacity when booking.

The windows look out towards the hills and mountains. A peaceful base for exploring Possagno, Asolo and Bassano del Grappa.
