Frequently asked questions

Where is Dai Squee?

Dai Squee is at Viale A. Canova 51, 31054 Possagno, in the province of Treviso, in the foothills of Veneto.

How many apartments are there?

The house has three apartments: Suite Max, Michele and Rosa e Romeo. Each has an equipped kitchen and a private bathroom.

Which apartment has a private terrace?

Suite Max has a private terrace with a sun deck. Rosa e Romeo has a south-facing terrace accessible from its bedroom.

How can I book directly?

Choose your apartment and dates in the booking calendar. When instant booking is available, you will see the total and terms before confirmation. Alternatively, contact info@daisquee.it or +39 349 4454604.

What can I visit nearby?

Possagno and the sites associated with Canova, Asolo, Bassano del Grappa, Villa Barbaro in Maser, the Brion Memorial, Monte Grappa and the Prosecco hills.

Do I need to pay straight away?

It depends on the options enabled by the property: card payment when booking, bank transfer or payment on arrival. Available options and terms are shown before confirmation.

Are there check-in times and cancellation terms?

Arrival times, any extra charges and cancellation terms must be agreed with the property and are communicated before confirmation.
